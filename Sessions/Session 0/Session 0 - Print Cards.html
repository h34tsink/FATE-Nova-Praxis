Session 0 — Tarot Print Cards

17 cards sized for tarot stock (70 × 120 mm / 2.75 × 4.75 in). Print directly: Ctrl/⌘ + P → set Margins: None, Paper: Custom 70×120mm, Scale: 100%, Background graphics: ON. Or "Save as PDF" and send to a printer that handles tarot stock.

Cutting layout: if printing on letter, use your browser's Pages per sheet or import the PDF into Affinity / Adobe and lay 4-up at 100% with bleed marks. 17 cards = ~5 sheets duplex at 4-up.

Rules · 1 of 5FATE
Action Types

Simple Action (×1)

Your main thing. Attack · Maneuver · Test · Block · Full Defense.

Free Action (×1)

Fast, no roll. Move 1 zone · take cover · draw weapon · short speech.

Supplemental

A second small thing at –1 to your primary roll. Hustle 2 zones (–2) · reload mid-action.

Instant

Any time, even off-turn.

Defense is always free — never costs your turn.

Rules · 3 of 5FATE
The Compel Loop

This is the engine.

  1. GM compels your Aspect → "This complicates your life because…"
  2. Accept → +1 FP, narrate complication.
    Refuse → pay 1 FP, complication doesn't happen.
  3. Later, invoke an Aspect: spend 1 FP, name it, explain → +2 or reroll.
  4. Spin (3+ Shifts) → free Effect Aspect for the table.
  5. Ally invokes it free → chain begins.
Lean into compels. They're not punishment — they're fuel.

Rules · 4 of 5FATE
Invoking Aspects

Spend 1 FP. Name the Aspect. Explain why it helps. Get +2 or reroll.

The "Therefore" Test

I have [Aspect], therefore it helps me [do this].

If you need two leaps, it's a stretch.

Scope Rule (one per scope per roll)

  • 1 Character Aspect
  • 1 Scene Aspect
  • 1 Zone Aspect
  • 1 Equipment Aspect
  • 1 Consequence on target

Persistent (P) Aspects are exempt — invoke free, no scope limit.
