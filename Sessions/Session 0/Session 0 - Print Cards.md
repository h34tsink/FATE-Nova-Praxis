# Session 0 — Print Cards (Tarot Size)

Designed for **standard tarot stock: 2.75" × 4.75" (70 × 120mm)**. Each card below is sized to fit at 10–11pt body text with ~6mm margins. Layout in InDesign / Affinity / Canva — or print 4-up on letter paper, score, and cut.

**Card index:**

- **Drop 1 Fragments** ×3 — hand across the table during Scene 3
- **NPC Quick Reference** ×2 — Operative and Savant Handler, for Scene 5
- **PC Compel Reminders** ×7 — one per player; Disadvantage on the front
- **Rules Cheatsheets** ×5 — give to new players, leave in the middle for everyone

**Print tip:** Print the Drop 1 fragments on darker / aged stock if you can. Print the rules cheatsheets on lighter card so they're easy to read at a glance. PC compel cards work well in a translucent sleeve so the player can hold them face-up.

## D. Rules Cheatsheet Cards

For new players especially. Leave in the middle of the table.

---

### CARD: Action Types

**ON YOUR TURN, YOU GET:**

**Simple Action** (×1) — your main thing.
*Attack, Maneuver, Test, Block, Full Defense.*

**Free Action** (×1) — fast, no roll.
*Move 1 zone, take cover, draw weapon, short speech.*

**Supplemental** — a second small thing **at –1** to your primary roll.
*Hustle 2 zones (–2), reload mid-other-action.*

**Instant** — happens any time.
**Defense is always free** — never costs your turn.

---

### CARD: Stress & Consequences

**WHEN HIT:**
`Stress = Shifts + WR – AR`

**Absorb with stress boxes** (left to right). Boxes clear after the scene.

**Or take a Consequence** (becomes an Aspect):

| Slot | Absorbs | Recovery |
|------|---------|----------|
| Mild | 2 | 1 hour / 1 day |
| Moderate | 4 | 1 day / 1 week |
| Severe | 6 | 1 week / 1 month |
| Extreme | 8 | Permanent |

**Can't absorb?** Taken Out — OR **Concede** first (+1 FP, you narrate exit).

---

### CARD: The Compel Loop

**This is the engine.**

1. GM compels your Aspect → "This complicates your life because…"

2. **Accept** → earn 1 FP, narrate the complication
**Refuse** → pay 1 FP, the complication doesn't happen

3. Later, **invoke** an Aspect: spend 1 FP, name it, explain → +2 or reroll

4. **Spin** (3+ Shifts) → free Effect Aspect for the table

5. Ally invokes it free → chain begins

**Lean into compels.** They're not punishment. They're fuel.

---

### CARD: Invoking Aspects

**To invoke:** Spend 1 FP. Name the Aspect. Explain why it helps. Get **+2 or reroll**.

**The "Therefore" Test:**
> *I have [Aspect], **therefore** it helps me [do this].*

If you need two leaps, it's a stretch.

**Scope Rule** — one invoke per scope per roll:

- 1 Character Aspect
- 1 Scene Aspect
- 1 Zone Aspect
- 1 Equipment Aspect
- 1 Consequence (on target)

**Persistent Aspects (P)** are exempt — invoke them free, no scope limit.

---

### CARD: Movement & Zones

**ZONE-BASED — no grid, no inches.**

| Move | Cost | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Adjacent zone | Free Action | No penalty |
| Hustle (2 zones) | Supplemental | –2 to other action |
| Sprint (3+ zones) | Athletics test | Diff 2; each Shift = +1 zone |

**Within a zone:** you can act on anything in it without movement.

**Blocked / hazardous zone:** crossing may need a test. GM sets the difficulty.

**Cover:** taking cover in your zone is a Free Action — grants *Behind Cover* Aspect.

## Print Layout Notes

- **Drop 1 Fragments**: large quote text, generous spacing, low-fi or aged background
- **NPC cards**: dense info, mono spacing for the stress trackers
- **PC compel cards**: reserve a small box at the bottom for a Fate Point token to physically sit when compelled
- **Rules cards**: bold the headings, keep tables tight; these get glanced at, not read

If laying out 4-up on US letter, you can fit ~9 cards per side (4×3 with bleed). 17 cards = 2 sheets duplex.
